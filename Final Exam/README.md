## How It Works

The submitted report and question document accompany a notebook that reconstructs information from Minard-related campaign data. The notebook organizes cities, coordinates, troop counts, and direction into a DataFrame and creates figures from those records.

## Main Tasks

- Inspect the question document and submitted explanation.
- Review the data entered in the plotting notebook.
- Relate visual marks and encodings to the quantities being compared.
